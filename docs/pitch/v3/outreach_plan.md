# Outreach Plan

## ICP For The Next 30 Days

Pick one:

> Emerging seed managers and solo GPs deploying Fund I/II with $10M-$75M AUM, high founder inbound, and no dedicated internal platform or analyst team.

Why this ICP:

- They feel institutional-process pressure earlier than they can hire for it.
- They are closer to the founder and easier to reach than large firms.
- They do not have heavy procurement.
- A single GP can say yes to a historical pilot.
- They care about LP-facing process and decision discipline.

Do not target accelerators, studios, and established VC firms in the same motion. They are different products.

## Monday Action Plan

### Build The Target List

Create a spreadsheet of 50 targets:

- 20 emerging seed funds in Europe/UK/Ireland.
- 10 solo GPs or micro-funds.
- 10 fund-of-funds / LP operators who know emerging managers.
- 10 venture scouts or syndicate leads with fund ambitions.

Fields:

- Name.
- Fund.
- AUM/fund stage if known.
- Geography.
- Thesis.
- Warm path.
- Reason they might care.
- Contact channel.
- Status.

### Outreach Goal

Book 10 conversations.

Convert 3 into historical pilots.

Convert 1 into live shadow-mode usage.

## Why They Pick Up

Do not pitch "Aethria."

Pitch a painful artifact:

> "I am studying how emerging managers lose track of pass/monitor decisions. I built a prototype that turns 20 historical deals into a decision ledger and risk postmortem. I want to run it on your old deals and show you where your process was consistent, too strict, or too loose."

This is easier to accept than:

> "Try my AI venture platform."

## Pilot Offer

Name:

> 20-Deal Decision Postmortem

Input:

- 5 invested deals.
- 5 correct passes.
- 5 regretted passes or missed deals.
- 5 ambiguous monitor deals.

Output:

- Decision ledger.
- Risk register.
- Scoring/rubric consistency report.
- Stale-deal / ownership failure patterns.
- 2-3 example IC packs.
- Recommendation on how Aethria would fit their Monday meeting.

Time ask:

- 30-minute setup.
- 45-minute review.
- Optional live shadow mode.

Data posture:

- Historical only.
- Public sources where possible.
- User can redact sensitive details.
- Delete/export on request.

## First Paid Conversion Trigger

Do not charge until one of these happens:

- They use Aethria for a live partner meeting.
- They ask to run all new inbound through shadow mode.
- They export IC packs for more than 5 live companies.
- They record real pass/monitor/invest rulings in Aethria.

Paid pilot hypothesis:

- $500-$1,500/month for emerging managers during beta.
- Higher only after live workflow usage is proven.
