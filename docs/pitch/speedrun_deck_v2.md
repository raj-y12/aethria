## Slide 3 - Why Current Tools Fail

**Title**

CRMs remember activity. Funds need to remember judgment.

**Core message**

Affinity, Attio, spreadsheets, Slack, and memos can tell you that a company existed. They rarely preserve the firm's reasoning.

**Slide copy**

Current stack:

- CRM: who knows whom.
- Data vendors: what the company is.
- Notes: what someone thought that week.
- Memo: what was written for one meeting.

Aethria:

- Why this fits the mandate.
- What evidence is missing.
- What risks were acknowledged.
- Who ruled and why.
- Which past decision is precedent.

**Visual**

Simple table: "Activity record" vs "Judgment record."

**Talk track**

"Aethria can live beside the CRM. It is not trying to own contacts. It owns the investment reasoning."

## Slide 4 - The Workflow

**Title**

Queue -> risk -> ruling -> memory.

**Core message**

Aethria turns deal evaluation into a repeatable operating loop.

**Slide copy**

1. Fund thesis gates the pipeline.
2. Deals enter through scout discovery or on-demand lookup.
3. Reviewers score market, product, technical, execution, and defensibility.
4. The adverse register surfaces red flags and missing data.
5. Partner rules recommend Invest, Pass, or Monitor.
6. Humans record the final ruling.
7. The decision becomes fund memory.

**Visual**

One loop diagram. Keep it literal.

**Talk track**

"The atomic unit is the ruling, not the memo."

## Slide 6 - GTM Wedge

**Title**

Start with postmortems and shadow-mode IC packs.

**Core message**

We do not ask funds to trust us with the whole pipeline on day one.

**Slide copy**

Entry path:

- Run 20 historical deals through Aethria.
- Compare against known invests, passes, misses, and maybes.
- Move to shadow mode on live inbound.
- Produce IC packs for selected deals.
- Become the decision ledger once rulings are recorded.

Initial customers:

- emerging managers,
- solo GPs,
- micro-funds,
- accelerators and venture studios.

**Visual**

Five-step trust ladder: historical -> shadow -> IC pack -> decision ledger -> full queue.

**Talk track**

"Selling to VCs is hard because trust is the product. The wedge has to earn trust before asking for system-of-record behavior."
