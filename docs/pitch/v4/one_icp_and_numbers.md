# One ICP And Numbers

## Narrow ICP

Use one customer:

> Solo GP or two-person Fund I seed manager with $10M-$25M AUM, no full-time analyst, no formal IC process, and deal tracking spread across spreadsheet/Notion/CRM-lite plus WhatsApp/email.

Why this ICP:

- One person can say yes.
- They do not have a deeply embedded process to displace.
- Aethria can become the process rather than integrate with one.
- The product creates cadence instead of inheriting a Monday partner meeting.
- They care about looking institutionally disciplined to LPs.

Exclude for now:

- $50M-$75M funds with analysts and Affinity.
- Accelerators and venture studios.
- Multi-partner established firms.
- Funds with formal IC already running well.

## Habit Model

Do not anchor on "Monday partner meeting."

Anchor on:

> Weekly Deal Reset

For a solo GP, Aethria creates a 20-minute ritual:

1. Open unresolved queue.
2. See stale, adverse, and ready deals first.
3. Decide: pass, monitor, push, or assign next action.
4. Export IC/advisor packet only for serious deals.
5. Record the ruling.
6. Next week starts from memory.

This matches solo/two-person fund behavior better than formal IC.

## Number Logic

The deck should not claim "1,000 reachable managers" unless Raj has a list or a credible source.

Use this instead:

> First target map: 200 solo/two-person emerging seed managers by name.

If the map does not exist yet, say:

> Next 14 days: build the 200-fund target map and contact the first 50.

## Funnel Assumptions

Bottom-up pilot funnel:

- 200 named target funds.
- 50 contacted in first outbound sprint.
- 15 conversations.
- 5 historical-postmortem pilots.
- 1 live shadow-mode user.

Conversion assumptions:

- 30% conversation rate from warm/targeted outbound is aggressive but possible with intros and a sharp offer.
- 33% pilot conversion from conversations is plausible only if the 20-deal postmortem is clearly low-risk.
- 20% live conversion from pilots is the real test.

## Revenue Logic

Early paid pilot:

- $500/month for solo GP beta.
- $1,000/month once live shadow mode is used.
- $12K/year target ACV for first paid cohort.

First proof milestone:

- 10 paying funds x $12K ACV = $120K ARR.

Not venture-scale yet. Proof of wedge.

Expansion:

- 100 funds x $12K = $1.2M ARR.
- 300 funds x $18K = $5.4M ARR.
- Larger funds later require collaboration, integrations, retention, and security.

## Deck Number To Use

Use:

> 200 named targets -> 50 contacted -> 15 calls -> 5 pilots -> 1 live shadow-mode fund.

This is more credible than floating market numbers.
