A6The Review

The reviewers disagree. It stays on the record.

Opportunist — for
The wedge is real and capital-efficient. A defensible technical moat in a market pulling hard toward the next compute cycle. Early traction is thin but the direction is unambiguous; this is exactly the asymmetry the thesis is built to catch.
Skeptical — against
The moat is asserted, not proven. Defensibility scores a seven for a reason: no evidence of switching cost yet, and two funded competitors within eighteen months. Strong story, unproven execution. Monitor before conviction.

The partner engine logs the split rather than averaging it away — a 9 and a 7 remain a 9 and a 7.
