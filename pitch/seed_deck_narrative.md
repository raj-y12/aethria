# Recommended Seed Deck Narrative

## Narrative Arc

The strongest narrative is not "we help VCs source more companies." It is:

> Venture firms do not have a sourcing problem alone. They have an institutional judgment problem: attention is fragmented, conviction is inconsistent, and decision memory does not compound. Aethria is the operating layer that turns a fund's mandate into repeatable, auditable investment judgment.

Recommended sequence:

1. The problem is missed ownership of promising deals.
2. Existing tools organize data, not judgment.
3. Aethria is conviction infrastructure.
4. The product is a queue, mandate, scoring, risk, ruling, memory, and calibration system.
5. The wedge starts with venture dealflow but expands into fund intelligence infrastructure.

## Slide 6 - The Operating Loop

**Core message**

Every decision becomes structured institutional memory.

**Body copy**

Aethria separates system recommendation from human ruling. When a partner resolves a deal, the system records the decision, rationale, score snapshot, confidence, risk acknowledgement, and configuration version.

**Supporting bullets**

- Human rulings: Invest, Pass, Monitor.
- Score and confidence snapshots at the time of decision.
- Risk acknowledgements for adverse items.
- Precedent candidates for similar future companies.
- Fund memory capped and structured for reuse.

**Visual direction**

Loop diagram: `Review -> Recommend -> Human ruling -> Conviction state -> Fund memory -> Future queue`.

**Founder talking point**

"The compounding asset is not the memo. It is the structured history of how the fund decided."

## Slide 7 - Scoring And Risk Architecture

**Core message**

The system is designed for explainable judgment, not black-box confidence.

**Body copy**

Aethria extracts structured signals, normalizes them, applies weighted aggregation, runs multiple reviewer perspectives, resolves disagreement conservatively, and applies versioned partner rules.

**Supporting bullets**

- Dimensions: market, product, technical, execution, defensibility.
- Structured extraction -> normalization -> weighted aggregation.
- Skeptical, opportunist, and default review perspectives.
- Confidence-adjusted score when reviewers diverge.
- Versioned partner rules for invest/pass/monitor outcomes.

**Visual direction**

Technical pipeline diagram from signals to scores to recommendation, with "rules first, fallback only for grey zones."

**Founder talking point**

"The goal is not perfect automation. The goal is consistent, inspectable judgment that partners can challenge."
