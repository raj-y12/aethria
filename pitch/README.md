# Aethria Pitch Materials

Generated from a codebase audit of backend pipeline, queueing, settings, scoring, memo, conviction state, frontend UX, and public positioning.

Files:

- `codebase_audit.md` - what the product actually is, strongest differentiators, current gaps, and what to emphasize or hide.
- `seed_deck_narrative.md` - recommended seed-stage deck sequence with slide title, core message, body copy, bullets, visual direction, and founder talking points.
- `speedrun_deck_v2.md` - tighter A16Z Speedrun-style narrative rewritten around customer pain, product proof, GTM, founder fit, and moat.
- `gtm_moat_v2.md` - wedge, trust path, design partners, defensibility, and proof standards.
- `rejection_response_v2.md` - direct response to the seven rejection notes and how v2 changes the pitch.
- `positioning_variants.md` - category options, one-liners, opening hooks, market frames, strongest thesis, and strongest framing.
- `investor_objections.md` - likely objections, likely questions, weak points, and what to strengthen before fundraising.

Recommended headline framing:

> Aethria is conviction infrastructure for venture firms: a workflow-native system that turns a fund's mandate, rubric, risk posture, and decision history into an operating layer for institutional investment judgment.

Recommended Speedrun headline:

> Venture firms do not remember why they passed, why nobody pushed, or what changed since the last Monday meeting. Aethria is the deal command center that keeps every opportunity moving toward a clear owner, risk register, and investment ruling.
