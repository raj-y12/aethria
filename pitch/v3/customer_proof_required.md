# Customer Proof Required Before Application

## The One Thing That Beats The Deck

One real design partner running real or historical deals beats every slide.

Minimum bar before submitting to Speedrun:

- One named or describable fund agrees to run 20 historical deals through Aethria.
- At least one GP or decision-maker spends 45 minutes reviewing the output.
- You can quote one concrete workflow pain in their language.
- You can describe one deal-room scene without generic phrases.
- You can say what changed after using Aethria, even if the answer is "not enough yet."

## The Deal Story You Need

The opening slide needs one scene, not a list of familiar phrases.

Structure:

1. Monday partner meeting.
2. A specific company.
3. A specific owner or lack of owner.
4. A specific piece of missing evidence.
5. A specific failure mode.
6. What Aethria would have forced into the room.

Template:

> On Monday, `[Fund]` had `[Company]` in the meeting. Everyone liked `[specific thing]`. Nobody owned `[specific next step]`. The risk was `[specific risk]`, but it lived in `[Slack/Notion/memory]`, not the decision surface. Two weeks later `[specific consequence]`. The issue was not sourcing. The issue was that conviction had no operating home.

Do not use this template with invented details.

## Proof Ladder

### Level 0 - Product Only

Current state. Useful, but not fundable as a customer story.

Evidence:

- Working product.
- Pipeline, scoring, adverse register, rulings, IC pack.

Investor read:

- "Come back after customer proof."

### Level 1 - Historical Pilot

One fund runs 20 old deals.

Evidence:

- Aethria reconstructs known decisions.
- It exposes missed context or process gaps.
- GP says output would have changed meeting quality.

Investor read:

- "Founder can access customers and provoke a real conversation."

### Level 2 - Shadow Live Deals

One fund runs 5-10 live deals in parallel with existing workflow.

Evidence:

- Partner opens Aethria before or during meeting.
- Deals move to pass/monitor/invest faster.
- At least one adverse item changes the discussion.

Investor read:

- "This might become habitual."

### Level 3 - Decision Ledger

Fund records real rulings inside Aethria.

Evidence:

- Human decisions saved.
- Risk acknowledged.
- IC pack exported.
- Precedent reused.

Investor read:

- "Switching cost can begin."

## Anti-Handwave Checklist

Before presenting:

- Can you name the exact ICP for the next 30 days?
- Can you list 25 people/funds to contact?
- Can you show the email/DM you are sending?
- Can you explain why they will take your call?
- Can you show one actual customer quote?
- Can you show one real deal postmortem?
- Can you explain how the product enters the Monday meeting?
- Can you say what the fund currently uses instead?
- Can you define the first paid conversion trigger?

If the answer to any of these is no, the deck is still ahead of the customer.
