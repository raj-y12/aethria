# Founder Story Prompts

## Do Not Invent The Founder Slide

The current weak point is not wording. It is missing evidence.

The founder slide must contain at least one true, concrete story from Raj's own work.

## Founder Slide Shape

**Title**

I know what it looks like when expert judgment resists process.

**Body**

Use one specific story:

> At `[EY/client/project/team]`, I worked on `[specific operating process]`. The hard part was not the software. The hard part was that experts had judgment in their heads, exceptions in Slack/email, and no shared operating record. I learned that systems only work when they enter the meeting where decisions are made.

Then bridge:

> Aethria applies that operating lesson to venture: if the ruling happens outside the system, the memory never compounds.

## Questions Raj Must Answer

1. What exactly did you do at EY?
2. Did you build, audit, implement, migrate, automate, document, test, or operate a workflow?
3. What decision process did that workflow affect?
4. Who resisted it and why?
5. What did you learn about expert users?
6. What did you learn about controls, auditability, or exceptions?
7. What did you personally ship or own?
8. What metric, deadline, stakeholder, or artifact proves it?
9. How does that map to Aethria?
10. What part of VC do you still not understand yet?

## Acceptable Founder Claims

Only use claims that can be backed up:

- "Built the current product end-to-end."
- "Engineering graduate."
- "Worked in technology consulting."
- "Worked around structured operating environments."
- "Saw how expert workflows fail when judgment lives outside the system."
- "Need GP design partners to close the industry-context gap."

## Claims To Avoid Unless Proven

- "I designed enterprise decision systems."
- "I understand venture partner meetings."
- "I have deep VC workflow expertise."
- "I built controls for financial institutions."
- "I know how GPs decide."

## Stronger Honest Version

> I am not a career VC. That is the risk. The counterweight is that I have built the product, I understand structured operating workflows, and I am deliberately starting with historical postmortems so GPs can teach the system before trusting it with live pipeline data.
