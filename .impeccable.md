## Design Context

### Users
Primary users are VC analysts and investors using Aethria in a deal-flow context. They evaluate incoming startups, monitor portfolio outcomes, and tune autonomous scouting/review behavior.

### Brand Personality
The interface should feel confident, analytical, and high-signal while remaining calm, trustworthy, and institutional. Emotional goals: clarity under uncertainty, decision confidence, and operational control.

### Aesthetic Direction
Professional product UI optimized for investment analysis workflows. Avoid hype-driven visual treatments; prefer focused hierarchy, disciplined contrast, and high information legibility.

### Design Principles
1. Prioritize decision clarity over decoration.
2. Maintain institutional trust through consistency and restraint.
3. Preserve readability and scanability in dense information views.
4. Ensure controls feel reliable and predictable for repeated daily use.
5. Keep visual emphasis aligned with risk, confidence, and urgency signals.
